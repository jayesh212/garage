Privacy

We keep your name and email so you can sign in, the cars you add to your garage with the date you added them, and any purchase price you choose to enter.

Sharing

Your garage is private until you create a share link. Anyone with the link sees your first name and your cars. Purchase prices are never shown. Stop sharing at any time and the link stops working.

Rankings

The app shows how many collectors own each car and how your garage ranks against others. These are counts worked out from every garage; nobody else sees your name, your cars or your prices unless you share a link. A head-to-head comparison only works between garages whose owners have shared them, and never includes prices paid.

Deleting

Deleting your account in the app removes your profile and your garage straight away.

Photos

Car photos come from Wikimedia Commons under their free licences, with credit shown on each car.
